The Builder Behind Lift Veno

ആരാണ് ഇതിനു പിന്നിൽ?

Lift Veno is an independent project created and developed by Chriz. I’m a Cyber Forensics and Cybersecurity student from India with a strong interest in building practical technology, web applications, automation systems, mobile-focused products, and creative digital projects.

"My approach is simple: learn by building, test ideas practically, and turn concepts into real working systems."

Over time, I’ve built projects across web and mobile applications, progressive web apps, automation platforms, developer tools, AI-powered systems, and the SkyRealm Community.

Lift Veno is a new challenge in a different space: shared mobility. The project began with a simple question: How many people travel every day with an empty seat while someone else is trying to reach the same destination?

Before building the full platform, I’m researching the problem, talking to real people, collecting feedback, and testing whether Lift Veno can become a useful real-world solution.
