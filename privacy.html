Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Lift Veno is currently an independent research project by Chriz. We are not yet a fully operational platform. This concept privacy statement outlines how we handle the minimal data collected during this early validation stage.

1. Data we collect

Currently, we only collect data that you voluntarily provide through our travel survey or our early community waitlist. This may include:

  • Email address (for the waitlist)
  • City or general location
  • General travel preferences (e.g., whether you drive or seek transport)
  • Survey responses regarding your travel habits

2. How we use your data

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Understand travel behavior and validate the Lift Veno concept.
  • Communicate updates regarding the project if you joined the waitlist.

3. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. All data is securely handled by the project creator.

4. Contact us

If you have questions about this research phase or our data practices, please reach out to Chriz via the portfolio links on the homepage.
